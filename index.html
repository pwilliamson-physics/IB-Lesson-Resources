CSP

4 resources
  • Part A — Energy Balance & the Greenhouse Effect (Lesson 1)

    Introduces radiative energy balance and the inverse-square law as it applies to incoming solar radiation, finishing with a self-check quiz.

    CSP-worksheets/Worksheet_PartA_Lesson1.html
  • Part B — Energy Balance & the Greenhouse Effect (Lesson 2)

    Builds on Lesson 1 to develop the greenhouse effect model further, with an embedded self-check to consolidate understanding.

    CSP-worksheets/Worksheet_PartB_Lesson2.html
  • Part C — Synthesis Task: Reading Real Planetary Energy Budgets

    Students interpret real planetary energy budget data (based on Sylvia Knight's infographic) to synthesise their understanding of Earth's energy balance.

    CSP-worksheets/Worksheet_PartC_Synthesis.html
  • Part D — The Collaborative Sciences Project Brief

    The full CSP task: a 10-hour project timeline, a proposal template, two investigation options (surface albedo, or energy transfer under foliage), and guidance for sharing outcomes.

    CSP-worksheets/Worksheet_PartD_CSP.html

Currents and Circuits

10 resources
  • Modelling Electrical Circuits

    Self-marking workbook introducing how to model and represent simple electrical circuits, laying the groundwork before quantitative work begins.

    current-worksheets/00_modelling_electrical_circuits.html
  • Potential Difference and Kirchhoff's Laws

    Interactive notes covering potential difference and Kirchhoff's voltage and current laws, with worked examples and embedded practice questions.

    current-worksheets/01_potential_difference_and_Kirchhoffs_laws.html
  • Basic Circuits – Practical Workbook

    Practical workbook of series and parallel circuit problems for building fluency with the fundamentals of circuit analysis.

    current-worksheets/02_basic_circuits_workbook.html
  • DC Circuit Builder

    Drag-and-drop circuit simulator: build and wire up DC circuits on a canvas, then save or restore your circuit using a shareable code.

    current-worksheets/02_DC_circuit_builder.html
  • Charge, Current and Time

    Practice workbook on the relationship between charge, current and time, reinforcing the core Q = It equation.

    current-worksheets/03_charge_and_current.html
  • Basic Circuits Concept Check

    Short diagnostic quiz to check understanding of basic circuit concepts before progressing to resistance and Kirchhoff's laws.

    current-worksheets/03b_basic_circuits_concept_check.html
  • Resistance

    Worksheet covering resistance and Ohm's law, and how resistance depends on a conductor's properties.

    current-worksheets/04_resistance.html
  • Adding Resistors

    Self-marking workbook on combining resistors in series and parallel to find equivalent resistance.

    current-worksheets/06_adding_resistors.html
  • Potentiometer & Voltage Divider Simulation

    Interactive simulation comparing voltage control with a variable resistor (rheostat) against a potentiometer, to explore voltage-divider behaviour.

    current-worksheets/08_potentiometer_simulation.html
  • EMF and Internal Resistance

    Self-marking workbook on electromotive force, terminal potential difference and the internal resistance of real cells.

    current-worksheets/09_emf_and_internal_resistance.html
